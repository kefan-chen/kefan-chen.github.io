Job Market Paper

Growth at the Margin: Political Incentives and Firm Behavior in China
Sole author, September 2025
Abstract: This paper examines how China’s annual GDP growth targets—an essential feature of its economic governance—shape incentives for city leaders and influence firm-level output and resource allocation. Using survival models, bunching analysis, and a threshold-based strategy, I find that a one-unit increase in a city leader’s performance score—defined as the gap between actual and target GDP growth—raises the probability of promotion by 9–10%.
